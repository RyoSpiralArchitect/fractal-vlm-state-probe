### Reference Condition Crossings

Retain r5/r6 references, r7/r8 tests, all four arrangements and eight families.
At each learned site, propose a direction per family and reference arrangement a:

```
r[f,a] = unit(unit(z[f,r5,a]) + unit(z[f,r6,a]))
m[p,a,b] = cosine(z[p,b], r[own,a])
           - mean cosine to the other three same-class r[f,a]
fixed_change[p] = mean_b=1..3 m[p,0,b] - m[p,0,0]
matched_change[p] = mean_b=1..3 m[p,b,b] - m[p,0,0]
reference_difference[p] = mean_b=1..3 (m[p,b,b] - m[p,0,b])
```

Publish all 4 x 4 crossings, own/other scores, families and availability,
not a selected favorable diagonal. References use only the reference split;
no alignment is fitted to tests. Preserve learned amplitude floors and the
1e-12 cancellation rule. Aggregate three nonidentity arrangements, two
test pairs per family, then eight equally weighted families.

The a=0 row at learned sites must reproduce Note 0093 scores and missingness.
The identity matched_change - fixed_change = reference_difference must
close at every available pair and fixed-denominator aggregate. Freeze the
numeric tolerance in the executable plan and check it on analytic toys
before real scoring; do not select it from new real-score residuals.

Recovery with matched references would support condition-dependent
correspondence, not prove common orthogonal rotation. Failure to recover
would not prove absence of information under another decoder or metric.
Do not fit Procrustes transforms or optimize maps/layers from these outcomes.
